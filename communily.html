What's coming

A clear roadmap toward full fundraising support

Communily is being extended deliberately, in an order that keeps compliance-sensitive work — payments and Gift Aid — ahead of anything that depends on it.

Next

Donor records, separate from members

A dedicated donor profile — distinct from your existing beneficiary members — with communication preferences and Gift Aid declarations.

Then

Payments & Gift Aid

Stripe and GoCardless integration for donations and event payments, with automatic Gift Aid eligibility tracking and HMRC-format exports.

Then

Fundraising campaigns

Public donation pages, campaign tracking, and donor segmentation for targeted appeals.

Then

Communications & automation

Templated email campaigns and automatic triggers — a thank-you email sent the moment a donation lands.

Later

Event management

Registration, ticketing, and attendee communication, building on the existing activities and scheduling engine.

Xero and QuickBooks integration so donation and income data reconciles automatically.
